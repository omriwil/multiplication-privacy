Privacy Policy

App: MultiPlay (com.omriwil.multiplication)
Operator: Omri Willheim
Effective date: July 19, 2026

Summary

MultiPlay is a local practice quiz. It does not require an account, does not show ads, and does not use analytics or advertising SDKs. Progress preferences stay on your device unless you clear app or browser data.

Information we store on your device

The app may save the following locally (for example in web local storage):

  • Language preference (English or Hebrew)
  • Setup choices (score on/off, timer on/off, timer length)
  • Personal best score
  • Daily practice streak (dates and count)

This information is not sent to our servers. We do not operate a backend that collects quiz answers, names, emails, photos, location, contacts, or other personal profile data.

Information we do not collect

  • Account credentials or sign-in
  • Advertising identifiers for targeting
  • Analytics events or crash reports sent by us
  • Payment or purchase data inside the app (none today)

Network and third parties

On some platforms the app may load fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com). Those requests can expose standard connection data (such as IP address) to Google under Google’s Privacy Policy.

Your device maker or app store may process diagnostic or usage data separately under their own policies. That is outside this app’s control.

Children

The app is suitable for learners practicing multiplication tables. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because gameplay data stays on the device and we do not run accounts or cloud sync, there is no child profile stored with us.

Your choices

  • Change language and practice options anytime in the app
  • Clear local data by clearing the app’s storage, site data, or uninstalling

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date on this page. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
